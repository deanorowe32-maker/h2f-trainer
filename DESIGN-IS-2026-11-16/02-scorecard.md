# Scorecard

1. Good design is innovative — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: tap-to-mark body map, stations format, H2F five-domain readiness in one offline PWA.
   Justification: a clear refresh of the fitness-app pattern for Army H2F, but the parts (week planner, timer, medal case) follow known patterns.
2. Good design makes a product useful — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: Today → workout now one tap; Train tab still opens on 1+ screen of builder settings before exercises.
   Justification: the primary task completes, but adjacent builder surface adds steps on the Train route.
3. Good design is aesthetic — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: 17 font sizes and up to 19 spacing values, many 0.5 px apart.
   Justification: one visible look, but more than five inconsistencies in the type and spacing scale.
4. Good design makes a product understandable — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: "Aero/Anaer", "W1/M1/C1", bare "%" box, cut-off jump chips, three navigation levels.
   Justification: 2–3 controls are unclear and jargon is present.
5. Good design is unobtrusive — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: always-on header with brand + subtitle, bottom bar, segmented bar and chip row stack above content.
   Justification: chrome is visible but quiet; it doesn't fight content, it just takes room.
6. Good design is honest — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: accurate unofficial disclaimers; "powerful" and "perfect" once each.
   Justification: honest throughout, with minor inflation.
7. Good design is long-lasting — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: restrained palette and type; one trend marker (heavy pill/rounded-card styling).
   Justification: one dated marker.
8. Good design is thorough — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: empty, focus, dark, reduced-motion handled; Readiness text fails contrast (3.23:1) and has 8 small targets.
   Justification: one state family is rough (accessibility detail on Readiness).
9. Good design is environmentally friendly — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: 575 KB initial HTML/JS; no idle animation; dark mode and reduced motion honored.
   Justification: falls in the 500 KB–2 MB band.
10. Good design is as little design as possible — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: Train first screen: big title + subtitle + "How this works" + format chips; duplicated week strip; third-level chip rows.
   Justification: 3–5 removable or duplicated elements on the worst screen.

**Total: 16/30**
