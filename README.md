# H2F Trainer

Holistic Health and Fitness (H2F) trainer for Soldiers: weekly PT plans, AFT scoring, injury rehab by body map, sleep, nutrition, mind and spirit tracking, and fictional ribbons earned for consistency. Works offline and installs to your home screen.

- **Train:** build a weekly PT plan, run workouts with a timer, score your AFT, practice the CFT on one clock, and build your own goal test to share with your leader.
- **Integrator program:** a leader builds 8 weeks of training (warm-up, sets, rest, RPE, % of max and A/B/C group loads, deload weeks) and pushes it to the platoon as a message. Each Soldier sees their own weights, and their own plan is never changed.
- **Rehab:** tap where it hurts on the body map and get a phased PT plan, even for more than one injury at a time.
- **Readiness:** log sleep, meals, water, mood and reflections, with trends and evidence-based tips.
- **Challenges:** a new exercise challenge every day, a weekly total, a monthly miles-and-lift challenge, plus 10 holiday workouts from New Year to Christmas, all on the Today screen.
- **Ribbons:** earn 108 fictional ribbons for real habits, with stars each time you earn one again.
- **Unit:** join your leader's live squad with a join code and your weekly summary reaches them on its own, or send a summary code by hand. Leaders can roll up their whole unit. No accounts.

Your data stays on your phone. If you join a live squad, only the summary you choose is sent, and only your leader can read it. It works offline, and it installs like an app from the browser.

## Deploy

This is a static site with no build step. On Vercel: Add New → Project → import this repo → Deploy, with every setting left as it is.
